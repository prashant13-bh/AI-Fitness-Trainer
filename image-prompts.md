## Design Specifications

### Color Palette

- **Background:** `#0a0e1a` (dark navy), `#000000` (pure black)
- **Primary Accent:** Neon Cyan `#00ffff` to `#00d9ff`
- **Secondary Accent:** Neon Purple `#b000ff` to `#8b00ff`
- **Text:** White `#ffffff`, Light gray `#e0e0e0`
- **Cards:** Semi-transparent white with blur (glassmorphism)

### Typography

- **Headings:** Bold, Modern Sans-serif (like Poppins, Inter, or Montserrat)
- **Body:** Regular Sans-serif
- **Numbers/Stats:** Monospace for precision

### Effects

- **Glassmorphism:** `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`
- **Neon Glow:** `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 255, 255, 0.5)`
- **Gradients:** Linear gradients from cyan to purple

---

## Usage Instructions

1. Copy the prompt for the image you want to generate
2. Use it with your preferred AI image generator:
   - **DALL-E 3** (ChatGPT Plus/API)
   - **Midjourney** (Discord)
   - **Stable Diffusion** (ComfyUI/Automatic1111)
   - **Leonardo.ai** (Free tier available)
3. Generate multiple variations and pick the best
4. Save with appropriate filename (e.g., `banner.png`, `dashboard.png`)
5. Replace the Unsplash URLs in README.md with your generated images

---

## Additional Tips

- For best results, add `--ar 16:9` (Midjourney) or specify aspect ratio in other tools
- Use `--style raw` or `photorealistic` for more realistic renders
- Add `high quality, 4k, professional` to prompts for better output
- Generate 2-3 variations of each and pick the best one
